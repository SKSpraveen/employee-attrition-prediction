Compare Random Forest, SVM, KNN and XGBoost on Key Metrics

📊 Understanding the Metrics:
  • Accuracy: Overall correctness - percentage of correct predictions
  • Precision: When model says "WILL LEAVE", how often is it RIGHT? (avoid false alarms)
  • Recall: How many actual at-risk employees does it catch? (don't miss real cases)
  • F1 Score: Balanced score between precision and recall
  • ROC-AUC: Overall discrimination ability (0.5=random, 1.0=perfect)
